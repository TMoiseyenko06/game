how to play

  1. Mine ore. Place a Miner on a colored ore patch (iron, copper, coal, stone). It drops items facing its arrow.
  2. Move it. Chain Belts from the miner to a factory.
  3. Smelt. Feed ore into a Furnace. Select a recipe from its panel.
  4. Assemble. Feed plates and intermediates into an Assembler. Select a recipe.
  5. Deliver. Run the final belt into the Rocket Silo (place one anywhere) with rocket parts.

Tip: back-pressure is fine — if the next tile is full, items queue. Build parallel lines for throughput.
